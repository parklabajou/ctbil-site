Advancing Stem Cell
Therapeutics for a
Healthier Tomorrow

Our laboratory combines bioinformatics and experimental biology to develop next-generation regenerative therapeutics — spanning extracellular vesicle therapeutics, organoid-based disease modeling, and stem cell-derived immune cell therapeutics. By leveraging bioinformatics-driven insights, we accelerate cell-free and precision regenerative strategies for tissue regeneration, age-related disorders, and cardiovascular disease.

Guided by scientific curiosity, we pioneer integrative, biomimetic platforms to enhance human health and longevity.

What we do

Research Focus

We combine computational and experimental approaches to develop regenerative medicine for tissue regeneration and cardiovascular disease.

Extracellular Vesicle Therapeutics

Engineering EVs and exosomes as cell-free carriers for therapeutic RNA and proteins in precision regenerative medicine.

Organoid-Based Disease Modeling

Building skin, vascular, and cardiac organoids and assembloids to model development & disease.

Stem Cell-Derived Immune Cell Therapeutics

iPSC-derived regenerative macrophages and immune cells for vascular repair and control of inflammation.

Anti-Aging & Cellular Rejuvenation

ROS/RNS-releasing platforms that delay cellular senescence and rejuvenate stem cells.
