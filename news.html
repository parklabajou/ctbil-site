Great work, Minseo! 👏

Hyemin received a poster award at the 2026 KOBRA Conference on May 28, 2026. Her talk, "A Gastrostable Hybrid Carrier of Milk Extracellular Vesicles and Curcumin Nanovesicles for Oral miRNA Delivery," presented a hybrid carrier combining milk-derived extracellular vesicles with curcumin nanovesicles designed to survive the harsh gastric environment and deliver miRNA orally. Well done, Hyemin! 👏

Hyemin delivered an oral presentation at the 2026 Conference of the Korean Society for Biomaterials, held in Magok, Seoul, on March 27, 2026. Congratulations on the oral presentation, Hyemin! 👏
