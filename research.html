What We Do

We integrate bioinformatics and experimental biology to engineer regenerative therapeutics across three connected frontiers.

Area 01

Regenerative Immune Therapeutics

We develop treatments for chronic autoimmune and inflammatory diseases including psoriasis, atherosclerosis, and inflammatory bowel disease.

Modulating the immune microenvironment to suppress pathological inflammation and promote tissue repair and regeneration.

Our two core strategies are immunoregenerative macrophage therapies and extracellular vesicle-based gene delivery systems.

Area 02

Extracellular Vesicle Engineering

We design next-generation gene and RNA therapies through engineered extracellular vesicles capable of delivering therapeutic molecules such as miRNA to target tissues.

Loading engineered EVs with therapeutic miRNAs to suppress cancer progression and metastasis.

The work integrates bioinformatics to identify disease-relevant genetic targets and regulatory pathways.

ExosomesmiRNA deliveryCancerBioinformatics
Area 03

Organoid Engineering

We build skin, vascular, and cardiac organoids to model tissue-specific biology and disease.

Skin, vascular, and cardiac organoid models, together with advanced assembloid systems, recreate tissue-specific and multicellular interactions in health and disease.

Applications span skin aging, vascular development and dysfunction, and cardiac tissue modeling through integrated tissue engineering and computational analyses.
